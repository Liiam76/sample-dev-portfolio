I like the unglamorous parts of software: ledgers that balance to the cent, sync engines that survive a dropped signal, and command-line tools that save a team an hour every week.

At Paystride I rebuilt the payout reconciliation service and failed payouts dropped 38% in one quarter. Before that I wrote the sync engine behind Classbase's attendance app, which 1,200 schools use on phones that lose signal every afternoon.

Outside work I organise a monthly JavaScript meetup in Lagos and maintain a few open source tools.
